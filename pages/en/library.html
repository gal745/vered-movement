My library

Books, podcasts, videos and people I've learned from – and recommend to anyone who wants to go deeper.

A lot of the change happens between sessions: in what you read, what you listen to on the way to work, and what you try at home. I've gathered here the things I keep coming back to and recommend to my clients.

This page is updated from time to time. If you've come across something you think belongs here, send it to me – I'd love to see it.
