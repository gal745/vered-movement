Accessibility

Accessibility statement

We care that this website is usable by everyone, including people with disabilities.

What was done on this site

  • The site is built with standards-compliant code, a clear heading hierarchy and semantic markup.
  • The whole site can be navigated by keyboard, and every page has a “skip to content” link.
  • A clear focus indicator follows keyboard navigation.
  • Text and background keep a readable contrast ratio, and text can be enlarged in the browser without losing content.
  • Decorative images carry no redundant alternative text; meaningful images are described.
  • The site works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • For visitors whose system is set to reduce motion, animations on the site are switched off.

Accessibility of the sessions and the studio

A description of the physical accessibility of the studio and garden will be added here.

Sessions are adapted to each person, including practice while seated or with support. If you need a particular adjustment, tell me in advance and we'll find a way.

Known limitations

Content embedded from third parties – such as the Google map or social media pages – may not be fully accessible, as it is outside our control. Location and contact details also appear as plain text on the site.
